## 🌟 The $14.2 Billion Crisis & Our Problem Statement
Every year, consumers and businesses lose **$14.2 Billion** to predatory recurring charges, stealth subscription rate increases, and unauthorized billing errors. 
- Over 85% of legitimate disputes under $100 are abandoned by consumers due to 45-minute phone hold times and complicated bank dispute forms.
- Generic chatbots (ChatGPT) only offer passive advice.
- Unconstrained autonomous bots are hazardous: an inaccurate dispute can freeze a user's credit card or ruin credit history.

**AegisGuard** is an autonomous multi-agent swarm that analyzes bank statement data, extracts evidence from merchant terms, cross-references statutory consumer protection laws (FTC ROSCA, CFPB, Visa Reason Codes), and drafts certified dispute filings—**while strictly enforcing a deterministic Human Sovereign Risk Gate before any financial or legal filing is submitted.**

---

## 🤖 Multi-Agent Swarm Ensemble

1. **Forensic Auditor Agent**: Ingests Plaid/bank transaction ledgers, flags unnotified recurring spikes, and isolates zombie mandates.
2. **Legal & Regulatory Agent**: Matches statutory violations (FTC ROSCA 15 U.S.C. § 8403, CFPB Rule 1005.10(d), Visa Reason Code 13.5).
3. **Evidence & Merchant Agent**: Scrapes merchant terms, inspects cancellation friction/dark patterns, and compiles evidence dossiers.
4. **Adversarial Red-Team Verifier**: Audits evidence provenance, eliminates hallucinations, and calculates systemic Win Probability (96%+).
5. **Human Sovereign Risk Gate**: Halts execution, computes financial blast radius, and empowers the user to **Authorize Dispute**, **Edit Parameters**, or **Dismiss**.
6. **Execution & Clawback**: Dispatches dispute filings to banking portals and revokes recurring payment credential tokens.
